## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience: hiring managers, technical leads and recruiters evaluating Miguel Ángel Calzada Martín for software engineering roles. Secondary audience: peers and collaborators reviewing his public work. Visitors arrive with limited time and want to verify role, technical range, production experience and the quality of what he has shipped.

## Product Purpose

A professional portfolio that presents Miguel's work, experience and technical judgment, and makes it easy to contact him. Success means a visitor understands within the first viewport what he does, sees credible evidence of production work, and can open a project dossier or contact him without friction.

## Positioning

Full-stack engineering applied to real data and AI systems: APIs, microservices, data products and interfaces that run in production. The differentiator is the combination of shipped public products, hands-on infrastructure work and a clear engineering point of view, not a list of technologies.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse on desktop and mobile, often during a hiring or recruiting workflow. They may open the four public demos, inspect repositories, check experience and education, and then send an email or connect on LinkedIn or GitHub. The site is deployed on Vercel under miguelcalzada.com. The existing project demos are served through Next.js rewrites at /impactdb, /ai-lab, /sqlsense and /madrid-transit.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence over adjectives: show the system, the decision and the constraint.
2. Short path to proof: work and contact must be reachable within seconds.
3. Technical precision is part of the brand: dates, stacks, status and links must be exact.
4. No invented content. If a fact is missing, omit the claim rather than fill the gap.
5. The interface recedes; the work and the person lead.
